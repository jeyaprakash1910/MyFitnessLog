You can fix a workout you already finished.

- Tap any set on a past workout to correct its weight, reps or RPE
- Useful when you typed 80 and actually lifted 85, or tapped complete on a set you did not finish
- Clearing the RPE box removes an effort score you entered by mistake

The exercise name and its targets stay fixed. A finished workout should still say what you actually planned and did on the day, so you can change what you performed but not what you had set out to do.

Also fixed: tapping the effort button straight after typing your weight could occasionally save nothing at all, with no warning. That is gone.
